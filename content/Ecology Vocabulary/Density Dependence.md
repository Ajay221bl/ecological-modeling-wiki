# Density Dependence

**In one line:** density dependence means per-capita vital rates change with population density, usually falling as crowding increases competition, disease or predation.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Vital Rates]] · [[Population Density]]

## Start from zero
1. **Negative density dependence** stabilises populations around carrying capacity.
2. **Positive (Allee effects):** at very low density, individuals struggle to find mates, so growth falls.
3. **Density-independent factors** (a hard frost) act regardless of density.
4. **Detecting it statistically is hard:** observation error in counts creates spurious negative density dependence.
5. **Disease transmission** often rises with host density.

## Common confusions
- **"Weather events are density dependent."** Most act regardless of density.
