# Body Size

**In one line:** body size and morphology are an organism's physical dimensions, such as wing length, skull length or mass, which link to physiology, survival and reproduction.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Continuous Data]] · [[Variable]]

## Start from zero
1. **Structural size** (bones, wing, head-bill) changes little within a season; mass changes daily.
2. **Shipley et al. (2022):** adult tree swallows became structurally smaller over three decades, even though chicks grew larger in warmer nests and larger chicks were more likely to return; adult size trends were associated with warmer overwintering conditions.
3. **Bergmann's rule:** within a group, body size tends to be larger in colder places, the basis for predicting shrinking under warming.
4. **Measurement error** between observers matters; repeatability analyses (mixed models with observer effects) check it.
5. **Analysed with mixed models** because individuals are measured repeatedly over years.

## Common confusions
- **"Body size and body mass are the same."** Mass also reflects condition and changes quickly.
