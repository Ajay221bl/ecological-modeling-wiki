# Evapotranspiration

**In one line:** evapotranspiration is water lost to the air from soil and plants; potential evapotranspiration (PET) measures available energy (how much could evaporate), and actual evapotranspiration (AET) measures how much does, combining energy and water.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Explanatory Variable]] · [[Environmental Gradient]]

## Start from zero
1. **PET:** high where it is hot and sunny, regardless of rain. An energy measure.
2. **AET:** limited by both energy and water; high in warm, wet places. A productivity proxy.
3. **Water-energy hypotheses** of species richness use AET and PET as predictors.
4. **Diniz-Filho et al. (2003)** used PET, AET, range in elevation, annual mean temperature and minimum temperature to explain bird richness in western and central Europe, North Africa and the Middle East, comparing OLS and GLS.
5. **These predictors are smooth over space,** so they carry strong spatial autocorrelation.

## In the course
[[Diniz-Filho et al. 2003]].

## Common confusions
- **"PET is how much water plants use."** That is AET; PET is the potential if water were unlimited.

## Explain it back
- Which is limited by water: AET or PET?

*Pass test:* you can explain AET and PET as predictors.
