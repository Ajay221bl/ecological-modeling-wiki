# Vital Rates

**In one line:** vital rates are the per-individual rates of birth, death, growth and movement (survival, fecundity, recruitment, emigration) that together determine how a population changes.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Population Dynamics]] · [[Survival Rate]]

## The everyday picture
A bank account. Deposits (births, immigrants) and withdrawals (deaths, emigrants) set the balance. Vital rates are the interest and fee rates per pound in the account.

## Start from zero
1. **ΔN = B + I − D − E** (births, immigration, deaths, emigration; Powell and Gale Ch2 and Ch7).
2. **Survival and fecundity** are the core vital rates; recruitment and dispersal complete the picture.
3. **They vary with age, sex, density and environment,** which is what models of them try to explain.
4. **Estimated with** mark-recapture, known-fate studies, nest monitoring and counts of young.
5. **Sensitivity:** in long-lived species, adult survival usually matters more for growth than fecundity.
6. **Shipley et al. (2022)** linked tree swallow body size and mass to survival and reproduction to show selection on size.

## In the course
[[Powell and Gale 2015]] Ch1, Ch2 and Ch7. [[Shipley et al. 2022]].

## Common confusions
- **"Vital rates are counts."** They are per-individual rates or probabilities.
