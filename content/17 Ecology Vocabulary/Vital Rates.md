# Vital Rates

A bank account's balance is set by deposits and withdrawals, and its interest and fee rates are charged per pound in the account. **Vital rates** are the per-individual rates of birth, death, growth and movement, such as survival, fecundity, [[Jolly-Seber and POPAN Models|recruitment]] and emigration, that together determine how a population changes ([[Population Dynamics]], [[Survival Rate]], [[Fecundity]]). The bookkeeping is ΔN = B + I − D − E: births plus immigration minus deaths minus emigration (Powell and Gale Ch2 and Ch7). Survival and fecundity are the core rates, and recruitment and dispersal complete the picture.

Vital rates vary with age, sex, density and environment, which is what models of them try to explain ([[Density Dependence]]). They are estimated with [[Mark-Recapture|mark-recapture]], known-fate studies, nest monitoring and counts of young ([[Cormack-Jolly-Seber Model]], [[Known-fate Models]], [[Mayfield Nest Survival]]). In long-lived species, adult survival usually matters more for population growth than fecundity.

[[Shipley et al. 2022]] linked tree swallow [[Body Size|body size]] and mass to survival and reproduction to show selection on size ([[Powell and Gale 2015]] Ch1, Ch2 and Ch7). Vital rates are not counts; they are per-individual rates or probabilities.
