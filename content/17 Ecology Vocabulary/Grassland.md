# Grassland
**In one line:** A grassland is open land dominated by grasses and wildflowers, with few or no trees, kept that way by dry climate, fire, grazing, mowing, or a mix of these.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Biome]] (why climate alone does not explain Arkansas grasslands) · [[Ecosystem]]

## Start from zero
Picture a hayfield in summer: knee-high grass, scattered purple and yellow flowers, insects buzzing, and no trees except at the far edge. That is the everyday picture of a **grassland**. In North America a natural grassland is usually called a **prairie**.

A few words worth knowing:
- **Grasses (graminoids):** plants with narrow leaves and jointed stems, plus grass-like sedges and rushes.
- **Forbs:** non-grass flowering plants, the "wildflowers" (coneflowers, milkweed, goldenrod).
- **Perennial:** a plant that lives for years, regrowing from roots each spring. Native prairie plants are mostly perennials with deep roots.
- **Warm-season grasses** (such as big bluestem, switchgrass, Indian grass) grow most in summer heat. Many native prairie grasses are warm-season.
- **Cool-season grasses** (such as fescue) grow most in spring and fall. Many pasture and lawn grasses are cool-season, often non-native.

## Why it exists
Why is a grassland grass and not forest? Trees grow taller and shade grass out, so in a wet enough place trees should win. Grassland persists when something stops them:
1. **Dryness:** in the western Great Plains, not enough rain for many trees.
2. **Fire:** grasses keep their growing points at or below ground and resprout after a burn; young trees are killed.
3. **Grazing and browsing:** bison and other animals ate and trampled seedlings.
4. **People:** mowing, haying and disking now do some of this job ([[Disturbance]]).

Remove all four in a humid climate and a grassland turns into shrubs, then woods ([[Ecological Succession]], [[Woody Plant Encroachment]]).

Grasslands matter because a whole set of birds, insects and plants is built for open ground, and most of the original tallgrass prairie has been plowed into cropland.

> [!warning] Verify
> The common statement that well over 90% of North American tallgrass prairie has been lost is widely repeated but comes from no course reading here. Check a source before citing a number.

## A tiny example
Lay a 1 m by 1 m square on the ground and estimate what fraction of the square each plant group covers when seen from above:

| Group | Cover |
|---|---|
| Grass | 65% |
| Forbs | 20% |
| Shrubs | 5% |
| Bare ground | 10% |

That is **[[Percent Cover]]**. Grass plus forbs (85%) dominate and woody plants are low, so this square is grassland. Satellites estimate the same thing for each 30 m pixel.

## In my project
- All five sites are **patchy remnant grasslands** surrounded by forest or farmland ([[Study Sites]], [[Patch, Matrix and Corridor]]).
- The field data record several grassland-like cover classes within 50 m of each point: native/warm-season grass (NG50), cool-season grass (CG50), grass mix (GM50) and old field (OF50), plus lawn (LA50), which is grass but not grassland habitat.
- The satellite data from the [[Rangeland Analysis Platform]] lump grasses and forbs that live for years into one group, **perennial forb and grass cover (PFG)**. [[Spatial Covariance]] compares PFG with tree cover ([[Plant Functional Groups]]).
- Arkansas grasslands get far more rain than the Great Plains (127 to 137 cm a year), so they depend on management to stay open ([[Grassland Restoration and Management]]).

## Common confusions
- **"Any grass = grassland."** A mowed lawn, a fescue pasture and a native prairie are all grassy but offer very different habitat. Height, density and plant species matter to birds.
- **"Grassland is empty land."** Native prairie can hold over a hundred plant species in a small area, and underground root mass can exceed what is above ground.
- **"Grassland = savanna."** A savanna has scattered trees over grass. It is in between grassland and woodland.
- **"Once grassland, always grassland."** In a humid climate a grassland is a state that must be maintained.
