# Density Dependence

**Density dependence** means per-capita vital rates change with population density, usually falling as crowding increases competition, disease or predation ([[Vital Rates]], [[Population Density]]). Negative density dependence stabilises populations around [[Carrying Capacity]]. Positive density dependence, an Allee effect, happens at very low density, when individuals struggle to find mates and growth falls.

Density-independent factors, such as a hard frost, act regardless of density. Disease transmission often rises with host density. Detecting density dependence statistically is hard, because observation error in counts creates spurious negative density dependence: a count that is too high by chance is followed by an apparent decline ([[Observation Error]], [[Logistic Growth Model]]).

Logistic examples appear in [[Hobbs and Hooten 2015]]. Weather events are not density dependent; most act regardless of density.
