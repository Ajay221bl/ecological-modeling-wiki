# Canopy Cover

**Canopy or tree cover** is the proportion of ground covered by tree crowns when viewed from above, a common [[Predictor Variable|predictor]] of habitat for birds, plants and mammals ([[Proportion]], [[Habitat Structure]]). It runs from 0 to 1, or 0 to 100%, so as a response it suits beta regression or binomial models ([[Beta Regression]], [[Logistic Regression|Binomial GLM]]). It is measured with densiometers, photos, lidar or satellite products.

As a predictor it is often nonlinear: birds of open grassland vanish quickly as cover rises ([[Woody Encroachment]], [[Generalized Additive Model]]). It is collinear with [[Tree Density|tree density]], basal area and DBH ([[Diameter at Breast Height]], [[Collinearity]]).

It is a predictor in the lab datasets, including quail counts and tree cover in the GAM lab ([[Course Website]]). Canopy cover is not tree density: a few large trees can cover as much as many small ones.
