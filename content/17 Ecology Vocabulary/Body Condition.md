# Body Condition

**Body condition** is an animal's energy or nutritional state, usually measured as body mass adjusted for structural size, and it affects survival and reproduction ([[Body Size]], [[Linear Regression|Regression]]). Mass alone mixes size and condition, since a big bird can be heavy but lean. Condition indices use residuals of mass on a size measure, or a scaled mass index, and each has assumptions.

In models, put mass and a size measure in together rather than using a ratio, which can mislead ([[Collinearity]]). [[Shipley et al. 2022]] found male tree swallow breeding mass fell by 4% while female mass did not change, possibly because lighter females raise fewer offspring ([[Fecundity]]). Condition is measured repeatedly within individuals, so mixed models with individual ID are standard ([[Random Intercept]]).

Examples appear in Shipley et al. and [[Harrison et al. 2018]]. Mass divided by length is not a clean condition index: ratios can introduce spurious correlations with size.
