# Latitudinal Gradient

**In one line:** a latitudinal gradient is a systematic change in a biological pattern with latitude, most famously the increase in species richness from the poles to the tropics.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Environmental Gradient]] · [[Species Richness]]

## Start from zero
1. **Latitude is a proxy,** not a cause: temperature, energy, season length and history all change with it.
2. **Diniz-Filho et al. (2003):** bird richness showed a quadratic north-south gradient across their study region.
3. **Christensen et al. (2020):** the effect of drought on hemorrhagic disease depended on latitude (a drought × latitude interaction), with disease reports expanding northward.
4. **As a covariate,** latitude often absorbs broad spatial trends, like a trend surface.

## In the course
[[Diniz-Filho et al. 2003]]. [[Christensen et al. 2020]]. [[Shipley et al. 2022]] (Bergmann's rule).

## Common confusions
- **"Latitude explains richness."** It describes where richness changes; the explanation lies in variables that vary with latitude.

*Pass test:* you can interpret latitude as a predictor.
