# Exclosure

If you want to know how much your dog digs up the garden, fence off one bed and compare it with the rest after a summer. An **exclosure** is a fenced plot that keeps animals out, so that comparing plants inside and outside the fence shows what those animals do to the vegetation. The word is the mirror of "enclosure": an enclosure keeps animals in, an exclosure keeps them out. The fence height and mesh decide *which* animals are excluded. A low barbed-wire fence stops cattle but lets deer and elk jump over; a tall woven-wire fence stops all large hoofed animals ([[Grazing]]).

Homework 1 uses exactly this design. In the [[Riparian Zone|riparian]] meadows of the Valles Caldera, each site has three plots side by side: C, a control with no fence; LE, a livestock exclosure that keeps cattle out; and UE, an ungulate exclosure that keeps cattle, deer and elk out. Because the three treatments sit next to each other at every site, the comparison is made within a site, which removes most site-to-site differences in soil and water ([[Blocking]], [[Control Group]]). Comparing LE with C shows the effect of cattle; comparing UE with LE shows the extra effect of wild ungulates.

An exclosure is a [[Experimental Study|manipulative experiment]], so it supports causal claims better than comparing grazed and ungrazed places that happen to exist ([[Observational vs Experimental Study]]). Two limits matter when you model the data. The plots are measured every year, so the years are repeated measures on the same plots, not independent [[Replication|replicates]] ([[Repeated Measures]], [[Pseudoreplication]]); and a fence also changes shade, snow and trampling, so "fence effect" is not purely "no grazing".

## Flashcards

What does an exclosure do?
??
It fences animals out of a plot, so plants inside can be compared with plants outside to measure the animals' effect.
++++

In Homework 1, what do C, LE and UE mean?
??
Control (no fence), livestock exclosure (cattle out) and ungulate exclosure (cattle, deer and elk out).
++++

Which comparison isolates the effect of wild ungulates in Homework 1?
??
UE against LE, because both exclude cattle and only UE also excludes deer and elk.
