# Coarse Woody Debris

**In one line:** coarse woody debris is dead wood on the ground, such as fallen logs and large branches above a size threshold (often 7.5 to 10 cm diameter), which provides habitat and nutrients.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Habitat Structure]]

## Start from zero
1. **Measured** by line-intersect sampling (diameters crossing a transect) or by volume per plot.
2. **Habitat for** salamanders, small mammals, fungi, invertebrates and cavity nesters (snags).
3. **Changed by** fire (consumed or created), logging, storms.
4. **Data are skewed with many zeros,** so hurdle or Tweedie models can suit.

## In the course
Lab datasets ([[Course Website]]).

## Common confusions
- **"Dead wood is a sign of poor habitat."** It is a key resource for many species.
