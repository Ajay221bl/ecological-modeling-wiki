# MOC - Ecology Vocabulary

The ecology words the readings, labs and homework datasets assume you know, each explained from nothing.

## 1. Populations and demography
- [[Population Density]]: population density is the number of individuals per unit area (or volume), such as deer per square kilometre, which lets you compare places of different size.
- [[Vital Rates]]: vital rates are the per-individual rates of birth, death, growth and movement (survival, fecundity, recruitment, emigration) that together determine how a...
- [[Fecundity]]: fecundity is the number of offspring an individual produces in a period, such as eggs per clutch or fledglings per female per year.
- [[Carrying Capacity]]: carrying capacity K is the population size an environment can support in the long run, where births balance deaths because resources limit further growth.
- [[Density Dependence]]: density dependence means per-capita vital rates change with population density, usually falling as crowding increases competition, disease or predation.
- [[Tag Loss]]: tag loss is when a marked animal loses its mark, so it looks unmarked if recaptured, which biases mark-recapture estimates unless it is measured and corrected.
