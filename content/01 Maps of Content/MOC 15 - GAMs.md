# MOC 15 - GAMs

Oct 27 onward: generalized additive models let the data draw a smooth curve, and hierarchical GAMs let groups share or differ in that curve. Readings: Zuur et al. 2009 Ch3, Pedersen et al. 2019, Lawton et al. 2022, Noam Ross's GAM course.

## 3. Hierarchical GAMs

- [[Global Smoother]]: A global smoother is the single smooth curve shared by all groups in a hierarchical GAM, describing the average relationship.
- [[Group-level Smoothers]]: Group-level smoothers are the curves, one per group, that describe how each group's relationship differs from the global curve or stands on its own.
- [[Shared Wiggliness]]: Shared wiggliness means all group-level smooths use one smoothing parameter, so they are assumed equally complex, whereas individual wiggliness gives each...
- [[GAMM]]: A GAMM is a GAM with random effects or residual correlation structures, combining smooth terms with the grouping and dependence tools of mixed models.
- [[bam()]]: `bam()` fits GAMs to large datasets much faster than `gam()`, using methods designed for big data.
- [[Bayesian GAMs]]: Bayesian GAMs treat the penalised parts of smooths as random effects with priors, so the posterior gives full uncertainty for every curve and derived quantity.
