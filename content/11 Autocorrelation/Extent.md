# Extent

**Extent** is the total area, or time span, a study covers ([[Spatial Scale]]): one watershed is a small extent, a continent a large one. Larger extents include more environmental variation and more broad-scale gradients, and predictions beyond the extent are [[Extrapolation]].

Correlograms are usually read only up to about half the extent, because the far [[Distance Classes]] contain few pairs ([[Diniz-Filho et al. 2003]]).

Extent and [[Grain]] do not move together; they are chosen separately.
