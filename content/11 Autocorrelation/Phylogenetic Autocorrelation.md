# Phylogenetic Autocorrelation

**In one line:** phylogenetic autocorrelation means closely related species have similar traits because they share recent ancestors, so species are not independent data points.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Autocorrelation]] (the general idea) · [[Independence of Observations|Independence]] (what it breaks)

## Start from zero
1. **Distance here is time since two species shared an ancestor.**
2. **Body size, life history and many other traits show it.**
3. **Comparative analyses across species** must account for it, or sister species count as independent evidence when they are partly copies.
4. **Tools:** phylogenetic generalised least squares (PGLS), which is GLS with a correlation matrix built from the tree. Phylogenetic random effects in mixed models.
5. **Weak phylogenetic signal** suggests a trait is evolutionarily labile (McGill).
