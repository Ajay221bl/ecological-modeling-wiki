# Primary and Secondary Sampling Periods

**Primary periods** are the widely spaced sampling sessions between which a population or site can change, such as years or seasons; **secondary occasions** are the closely spaced surveys within a primary period, such as days within a trapping week or visits within a [[Breeding Season Timing|breeding season]], during which it is assumed closed ([[Robust Design]], [[Closure Assumption]]). Detection is estimated from the secondary occasions, and change from the primary periods.

Occupancy uses the same structure: visits within seasons, and seasons over years ([[Multi-season Occupancy]], [[Single-season Occupancy]]).

They are described in [[Powell and Gale 2015]] Ch13 and Ch15. Secondary occasions need not be one day apart; they must be close enough for closure, which depends on the species.
