# Super-population

Everyone who used a gym during a year is a different number from how many are there on any one evening. The **super-population** is the total number of different animals that were in the study area at any time during the whole study, including those that entered or left between occasions ([[Jolly-Seber and POPAN Models]], [[Open Population]]). It is always at least as big as abundance on any occasion ([[Abundance]]).

POPAN estimates it directly, and robust-design models can derive it ([[Robust Design]]). With [[Temporary Emigration]], the super-population includes animals that use the area but were not on it during some sessions. It is useful when management cares about all animals that use a site, such as a migration stopover.

Powell and Gale (Ch13) ask "How can I estimate the size of a super-population?" ([[Powell and Gale 2015]]). The super-population is not average abundance; it is the total of all distinct animals ever present.
