# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch5

> [!summary] In plain words
> Mixed models for nested data: random intercepts and slopes, induced correlation, the marginal model, REML versus ML, and a top-down model selection protocol, illustrated with RIKZ beach data and barn owl begging.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 5, "Mixed Effects Modelling for Nested Data". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Oct 8, mixed models lab (my addition).

## What it's about
Builds from the RIKZ beach data (two-stage analysis, then random intercepts and slopes), shows how random effects induce correlation within groups, explains ML and REML, compares good and bad model selection, validates the model, and analyses nestling barn owl begging.
