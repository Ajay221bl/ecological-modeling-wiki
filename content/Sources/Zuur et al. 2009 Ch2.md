# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch2

> [!summary] In plain words
> Linear regression's assumptions (normality, homogeneity, independence, fixed X, correct model) are often violated by ecological data, and each violation points to a different extension.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 2, "Limitations of Linear Regression Applied on Ecological Data". Springer, New York.
