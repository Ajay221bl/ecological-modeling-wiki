# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch9

> [!summary] In plain words
> Poisson GLMs for counts, the likelihood criterion, model selection, overdispersion and its fixes (quasi-Poisson, negative binomial), validation, and GAMs for counts.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 9, "GLM and GAM for Count Data". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Background for GLMs (Sep 1 to 17).

## What it's about
Treats Gaussian regression as a GLM, introduces the Poisson GLM with artificial and real examples, model selection with deviance and AIC, overdispersion, validation, quasi-Poisson and negative binomial GLMs, and a count GAM.
