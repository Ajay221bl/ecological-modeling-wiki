# Christensen et al. 2020

> [!summary] In plain words
> A logistic GLMM of hemorrhagic disease reports in eastern US counties shows drought severity predicts disease, more strongly at some latitudes.

**Citation:** Christensen, S. A., M. G. Ruder, D. M. Williams, W. F. Porter, and D. E. Stallknecht. 2020. The role of drought as a determinant of hemorrhagic disease in the eastern United States. *Global Change Biology* 26: 3799 to 3808. https://doi.org/10.1111/gcb.15095
**Syllabus week:** Tue Oct 6, "Mixed models: what, why, how?".

## What it's about
Uses 2000 to 2014 annual HD presence-absence for 1,830 counties in 23 states, with U.S. Drought Monitor severity, physiographic region, wetland cover, latitude and longitude as fixed effects, drought interactions with latitude and wetland, and state and year random effects (lme4), choosing models with AIC.
