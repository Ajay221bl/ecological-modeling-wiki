# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch4

> [!summary] In plain words
> Unequal residual spread can be modelled instead of transformed away, with variance structures in gls() and lme(), chosen with AIC on REML fits.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 4, "Dealing with Heterogeneity". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Background for mixed models (Oct 6 to 22).

## What it's about
Shows variance structures (varFixed, varIdent, varPower, varExp, varConstPower, varComb), how to choose among them, and a benthic biodiversity experiment worked through a protocol.
