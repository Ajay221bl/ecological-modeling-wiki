# Pedersen et al. 2019

> [!summary] In plain words
> An introduction to hierarchical GAMs in mgcv: five model types that combine a global smooth with group-level smooths of shared or separate wiggliness.

**Citation:** Pedersen, E. J., D. L. Miller, G. L. Simpson, and N. Ross. 2019. Hierarchical generalized additive models in ecology: an introduction with mgcv. *PeerJ* 7: e6876. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.6876
**Syllabus week:** Thu Nov 12, "Hierarchical GAMs".

## What it's about
Reviews GAMs and smoothing penalties, connects smooths to random effects, defines models G, GS, GI, S and I, fits them to CO2 uptake and other examples, and discusses choosing among them and computational issues.
