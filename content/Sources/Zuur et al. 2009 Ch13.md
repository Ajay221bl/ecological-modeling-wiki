# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch13

> [!summary] In plain words
> Generalized linear and additive mixed models for binomial and Poisson data, and the mathematics of fitting them (PQL, Laplace).

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 13, "GLMM and GAMM". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Mixed models (Oct 13 to 22) and GAMMs (Nov 10).

## What it's about
Sets the scene with a binomial GLMM (deer infection with *E. cervi*), then GLMMs and GAMMs for binomial and Poisson data, and the underlying mathematics.
