# Lawton et al. 2022

> [!summary] In plain words
> Hierarchical GAMs show that locust outbreaks follow prior vegetation growth differently at nested spatial levels, supporting hierarchical patch dynamics.

**Citation:** Lawton, D., P. Scarth, E. Deveson, C. Piou, A. Spessa, C. Waters, and A. J. Cease. 2022. Seeing the locust in the swarm: accounting for spatiotemporal hierarchy improves ecological models of insect populations. *Ecography* 2022: e05763. https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.05763
**Syllabus week:** Thu Nov 12, "Hierarchical GAMs".

## What it's about
Models desert locust and Australian plague locust outbreaks against preceding NDVI at three spatial levels (species range, geographic region, land unit), comparing HGAM structures with AIC, BIC and out-of-sample checks.
