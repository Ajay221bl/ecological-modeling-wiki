# Shipley et al. 2022

> [!summary] In plain words
> Three decades of tree swallow data show adults getting smaller even though warmer nests grow bigger chicks that survive better, so climate effects on body size are not simple.

**Citation:** Shipley, J. R., C. W. Twining, C. C. Taff, M. N. Vitousek, and D. W. Winkler. 2022. Selection counteracts developmental plasticity in body-size responses to climate change. *Nature Climate Change* 12: 863 to 868. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41558-022-01457-8
**Syllabus week:** Tue Oct 6, "Mixed models: what, why, how?".

## What it's about
Combines long-term monitoring and experiments with linear mixed models (individual and year effects). Adult structural size declined, associated with warmer overwintering conditions; male breeding mass fell 4% while female mass did not change.
