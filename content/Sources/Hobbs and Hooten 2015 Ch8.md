# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch8

> [!summary] In plain words
> After fitting, check the model (posterior predictive checks), then summarise the posterior: marginal distributions, credible intervals and derived quantities.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 8, "Inference from a Single Model". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 8, "Hello, Bayes!".

## What it's about
Covers model checking with posterior predictive checks and Bayesian p-values, marginal posteriors, credible intervals, and inference about derived quantities and predictions.
