# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch6

> [!summary] In plain words
> Hierarchical models split a problem into linked levels (data, process and parameter models), so latent quantities and observation error can be modelled explicitly.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 6, "Hierarchical Bayesian Models". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 8, "Hello, Bayes!".

## What it's about
Defines hierarchical models through factoring joint distributions, and works through example structures: models with observation error, random effects and latent states.
