# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch11

> [!summary] In plain words
> When counts have no zeros or too many zeros: zero-truncated, zero-inflated (ZIP, ZINB) and hurdle (ZAP, ZANB) models, and how to choose among them.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 11, "Zero-Truncated and Zero-Inflated Models for Count Data". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Beyond the course (later readings).

## What it's about
Explains zero-truncated data, sources of excess zeros (true and false zeros), mixture (ZIP, ZINB) versus two-part hurdle models, and compares Poisson, quasi-Poisson, NB, ZIP, ZINB, ZAP and ZANB with a flowchart.
