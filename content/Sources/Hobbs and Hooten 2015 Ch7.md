# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch7

> [!summary] In plain words
> How MCMC draws samples from a posterior that cannot be written down: Metropolis and Gibbs updates, burn-in, convergence and what to do with the samples.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 7, "Markov Chain Monte Carlo". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 8, "Hello, Bayes!".

## What it's about
Explains the logic of accept-reject Markov chains, Metropolis and Metropolis-Hastings steps, Gibbs sampling with full conditionals, and practical checks of convergence.
