# Brennan and Kuvlesky 2005

> [!summary] In plain words
> Grassland birds in North America have been declining for decades, and this review asks whether that adds up to a conservation crisis. It blames mainly the loss and breaking-up of grassland, and changes in how land is used and managed. It matters as the "why care" opening for Ajay's Introduction.

**Citation:** Brennan, L. A., and W. P. Kuvlesky Jr. 2005. North American grassland birds: an unfolding conservation crisis? *Journal of Wildlife Management* 69(1):1-13. https://doi.org/10.2193/0022-541X(2005)069<0001:NAGBAU>2.0.CO;2

## What it's about
A review arguing that grassland birds have shown long, widespread declines and asking whether this is a conservation crisis. It points to habitat loss, fragmentation and changes in land use and management as the main causes.
