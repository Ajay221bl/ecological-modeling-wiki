# Anderson and Burnham 2002

> [!summary] In plain words
> A list of common mistakes when using AIC: data dredging, mixing AIC with hypothesis tests, ignoring model uncertainty, poor candidate sets and misreading results.

**Citation:** Anderson, D. R., and K. P. Burnham. 2002. Avoiding pitfalls when using information-theoretic methods. *Journal of Wildlife Management* 66(3): 912 to 918.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 15, "Model selection".

## What it's about
Short, blunt advice: think hard before analysis, build a small set of hypotheses as models, do not mix AIC and p-values, report model selection uncertainty, and avoid post hoc dredging of many models.
