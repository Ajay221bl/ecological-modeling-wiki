# Oberpriller et al. 2022

> [!summary] In plain words
> Simulations on whether to treat a grouping variable with few levels as fixed or random: random effects stay reasonable for fixed-effect inference even with few levels, but variance estimates become unreliable.

**Citation:** Oberpriller, J., M. de Souza Leite, and M. Pichler. 2022. Fixed or random? On the reliability of mixed-effects models for a small number of levels in grouping variables. *Ecology and Evolution* 12: e9062. https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.9062
**Syllabus week:** Folder reading supporting mixed models.

## What it's about
Simulates mixed models with few grouping levels and compares fixed and random treatment for Type I error, power and singular fits, giving practical advice for small numbers of levels.
