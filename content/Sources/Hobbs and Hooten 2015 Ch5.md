# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch5

> [!summary] In plain words
> Bayes' theorem as the engine of inference: the posterior is proportional to likelihood times prior, sometimes available in closed form with conjugate priors, and priors deserve careful thought.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 5, "Simple Bayesian Models". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 8, "Hello, Bayes!".

## What it's about
Derives Bayes' theorem, connects it to likelihood, finds posteriors in closed form with conjugate pairs, and discusses kinds of priors (vague, informative, conjugate).
