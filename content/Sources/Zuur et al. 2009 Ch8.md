# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch8

> [!summary] In plain words
> A tour of the distributions behind GLMs (normal, Poisson, negative binomial, gamma, Bernoulli and binomial) and zero-truncated counts.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 8, "Meet the Exponential Family". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Background for linear models and GLMs (Sep 1 to 3).

## What it's about
Introduces each distribution with its mean-variance relationship and ecological uses, then the natural exponential family that unifies them.
