# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch3

> [!summary] In plain words
> When relationships curve, additive models replace straight lines with smoothers, with the amount of smoothing chosen by cross-validation, and collinearity still matters.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 3, "Things Are Not Always Linear; Additive Modelling". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Oct 27, generalized additive models.

## What it's about
Introduces additive modelling with LOESS and splines, technical details of GAMs in mgcv, a bioluminescence example for two stations, dealing with collinearity, and inference.
