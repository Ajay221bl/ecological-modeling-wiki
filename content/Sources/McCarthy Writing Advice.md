# McCarthy Writing Advice

> [!summary] In plain words
> Novelist Cormac McCarthy's advice for scientific writing, as told by Van Savage and Pamela Yeh: keep it simple, pick a theme and two or three points, one message per paragraph, short direct sentences, and cut anything the reader does not need.

**Citation:** Savage, V., and P. Yeh. 2019. Novelist Cormac McCarthy's tips on how to write a great science paper. *Nature* 574: 441 to 442. (Printed under the column heading "Tips from a Pulitzer prizewinner".)
**Syllabus week:** Tue Nov 24, "Writing results".

## What it's about
Short rules: minimalism, a single thread, one message per paragraph, short sentences with few transition words, no footnotes or jargon, a personal tone, concrete examples, equations set apart, reading aloud, and dealing calmly with reviewers.
