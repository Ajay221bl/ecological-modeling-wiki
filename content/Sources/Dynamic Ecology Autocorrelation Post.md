# Dynamic Ecology Autocorrelation Post

> [!summary] In plain words
> Everything in ecology is autocorrelated; a blog post arguing that autocorrelation is often a signal to model and understand, not just a nuisance to correct.

**Citation:** McGill, B. 2013. Autocorrelation: friend or foe? *Dynamic Ecology* blog, posted 2 October 2013.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Oct 1, autocorrelation lecture and lab.

## What it's about
Starts from Tobler's first law, notes that ecological variables are fields with spatial structure, and discusses when autocorrelation biases inference and when it is information about process and scale.
