# Zuur et al. 2009 Appendix A

> [!summary] In plain words
> A worked refresher of everything the book assumes: data exploration, linear regression, model selection and validation, and a first additive model, on one dataset.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Appendix A, "Required Pre-knowledge: A Linear Regression and Additive Modelling Example". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Oct 8, mixed models lab (my addition).

## What it's about
Runs the full cycle on a single dataset: explore (outliers, collinearity, relationships), fit a linear regression, select terms, validate with residual plots, and try an additive model when the residuals show non-linearity.
