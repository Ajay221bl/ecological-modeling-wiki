# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch6

> [!summary] In plain words
> Temporal correlation in regression residuals and how to model it with correlation structures such as AR(1) and ARMA in gls().

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 6, "Violation of Independence – Part I". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Oct 1, autocorrelation lecture and lab.

## What it's about
Covers temporal correlation in linear regression, correlation structures for multivariate time series, and owl sibling negotiation data.
