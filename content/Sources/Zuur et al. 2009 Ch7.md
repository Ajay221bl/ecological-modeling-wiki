# Zuur et al. 2009 Ch7

> [!summary] In plain words
> Spatial correlation: detect it with variograms and bubble plots, model it with spatial correlation functions, and remember it may signal a missing covariate.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Ch. 7, "Violation of Independence – Part II". Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Oct 1, autocorrelation lecture and lab.

## What it's about
Tools to detect violation of independence, spatial correlation structures (exponential, Gaussian, spherical, linear, ratio), Hawaiian birds, nitrogen isotopes in whales, spatial correlation due to a missing covariate (Section 7.5), and short godwit time series.
