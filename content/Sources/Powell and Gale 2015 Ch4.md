# Powell and Gale 2015 Ch4

> [!summary] In plain words
> AIC as a practical tool for comparing models fitted to the same data: penalise the maximised log-likelihood by the number of parameters, rank models, and weigh the evidence with ΔAIC and Akaike weights.

**Citation:** Powell, L. A., and G. A. Gale. 2015. *Estimation of Parameters for Animal Populations: A Primer for the Rest of Us.* Caught Napping Publications, Lincoln, NE. Ch. 4, "AIC and model selection".
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 15, "Model selection".

## What it's about
Explains why the most complex model is not automatically best, defines AIC and AICc, shows how to build a candidate set from hypotheses, and how to read ΔAIC, weights and model averaging, with examples from survival and population studies.
