# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch4

> [!summary] In plain words
> Likelihood turns a probability model around: with the data fixed, it measures how well each parameter value explains them, giving profiles, maximum likelihood estimates and a bridge to Bayes.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 4, "Likelihood". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Background for Tue Sep 8, "Hello, Bayes!" (not separately assigned).

## What it's about
Defines likelihood functions, likelihood profiles and maximum likelihood, and shows how prior information can enter a likelihood analysis, setting up Bayes' theorem in Ch5.
