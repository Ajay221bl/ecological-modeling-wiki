# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch3

> [!summary] In plain words
> The probability rules every Bayesian model rests on: conditional, joint and marginal probability, independence, factoring joint distributions into conditionals, and the common distributions with their moments.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 3, "Principles of Probability". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Sep 3, linear models and probability distributions lab.

## What it's about
Explains why first principles matter for building models, sets out the rules of probability, shows how to factor a joint probability into a chain of conditionals (the basis of hierarchical models), and surveys discrete and continuous distributions, including moment matching to turn a mean and variance into distribution parameters.
