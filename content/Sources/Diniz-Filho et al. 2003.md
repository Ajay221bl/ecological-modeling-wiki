# Diniz-Filho et al. 2003

> [!summary] In plain words
> Spatial autocorrelation in macroecological data need not bias regression coefficients, but it changes standard errors and which predictors look important at different spatial scales.

**Citation:** Diniz-Filho, J. A. F., L. M. Bini, and B. A. Hawkins. 2003. Spatial autocorrelation and red herrings in geographical ecology. *Global Ecology and Biogeography* 12: 53 to 64.
**Syllabus week:** Thu Oct 1, autocorrelation lecture and lab.

## What it's about
Analyses bird species richness across western and central Europe, North Africa and the Middle East against AET, PET, range in elevation, annual mean temperature and minimum temperature. Correlograms show strong spatial structure; OLS and GLS give different emphasis, with GLS de-emphasising predictors with long-distance clinal structure.
