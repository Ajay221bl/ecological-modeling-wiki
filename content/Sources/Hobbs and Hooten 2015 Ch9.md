# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch9

> [!summary] In plain words
> Comparing and combining Bayesian models with predictive scores: deviance, DIC, WAIC, cross-validation and model averaging, plus selection through priors.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 9, "Inference from Multiple Models". Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 22, model selection and diagnostics: Bayesian (my addition).

## What it's about
Frames model comparison as predicting new data, introduces scoring rules and information criteria (DIC, WAIC), cross-validation, posterior predictive loss, Bayes factors and model probabilities, and model averaging and regularisation.
