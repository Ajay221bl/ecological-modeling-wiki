# Guélat and Kéry 2018

> [!summary] In plain words
> Simulations and a Swiss bird case study show that imperfect detection and spatial autocorrelation both degrade species distribution maps, especially together, and spatial N-mixture models fix both.

**Citation:** Guélat, J., and M. Kéry. 2018. Effects of spatial autocorrelation and imperfect detection on species distribution models. *Methods in Ecology and Evolution* 9: 1614 to 1625. https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210X.12983
**Syllabus week:** Folder reading supporting autocorrelation and occupancy.

## What it's about
Compares a Poisson GLM, a non-spatial N-mixture model, and N-mixture models with CAR random effects or two-dimensional splines, on simulated data and on Common Redstart data from the Swiss Breeding Bird Atlas validated against independent monitoring.
