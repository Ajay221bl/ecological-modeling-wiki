# Harrison et al. 2018

> [!summary] In plain words
> A practical guide to mixed models and multi-model inference in ecology: what random effects do, how to choose and check them, and the many pitfalls of model selection and averaging.

**Citation:** Harrison, X. A., L. Donaldson, M. E. Correa-Cano, J. Evans, D. N. Fisher, C. E. D. Goodwin, B. S. Robinson, D. J. Hodgson, and R. Inger. 2018. A brief introduction to mixed effects modelling and multi-model inference in ecology. *PeerJ* 6: e4794. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.4794
**Syllabus week:** Tue Oct 6, "Mixed models: what, why, how?".

## What it's about
Explains random intercepts and slopes, nested and crossed effects, how many levels a random effect needs, model checking (overdispersion, R², residuals), and model selection and averaging, including warnings about stepwise deletion and Akaike weights as variable importance.
