# Johnson and Omland 2004

> [!summary] In plain words
> An overview of model selection as an alternative to null hypothesis testing: competing models as hypotheses, compared with likelihood-based criteria and combined by model averaging.

**Citation:** Johnson, J. B., and K. S. Omland. 2004. Model selection in ecology and evolution. *Trends in Ecology and Evolution* 19(2): 101 to 108.
**Syllabus week:** Model selection unit (Sep 15 to 22).

## What it's about
Describes the steps of model selection (generate hypotheses, fit, compare with AIC, BIC or likelihood ratio tests, weigh the evidence, average), and compares it with null hypothesis testing.
