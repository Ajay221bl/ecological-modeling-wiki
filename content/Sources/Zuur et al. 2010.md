# Zuur et al. 2010

> [!summary] In plain words
> A step-by-step protocol to explore data before modeling, so outliers, heterogeneity, non-normality, zeros, collinearity, odd relationships, interactions and dependence are found before they cause Type I or II errors.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, and C. S. Elphick. 2010. A protocol for data exploration to avoid common statistical problems. *Methods in Ecology and Evolution* 1: 3 to 14. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2041-210X.2009.00001.x
**Syllabus week:** Tue Aug 25, "Data exploration".

## What it's about
Eight steps with plots for each: outliers (boxplots, Cleveland dotplots), homogeneity, normality, zero trouble, collinearity (VIF), relationships, interactions (coplots), independence. It warns against using exploration to generate the hypotheses you then test on the same data, and asks that every exploration step be reported.
