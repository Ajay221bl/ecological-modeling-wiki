# Zuur et al. 2009

> [!summary] In plain words
> A hands-on book that starts from linear regression's limits and adds one tool at a time: GAMs, variance structures, mixed models, correlation structures, GLMs, zero-inflated models, GEE and GLMMs, each with real ecological data and R code.

**Citation:** Zuur, A. F., E. N. Ieno, N. J. Walker, A. A. Saveliev, and G. M. Smith. 2009. *Mixed Effects Models and Extensions in Ecology with R.* Springer, New York.
**Syllabus week:** Ch5 and Appendix A are my additions for mixed models (Oct 8); Ch3 is assigned for GAMs (Oct 27).

## What it's about
Each chapter fixes one violated assumption of linear regression. The book's habit of data exploration, model validation by residual plots, and a top-down protocol for mixed models runs through this wiki.
