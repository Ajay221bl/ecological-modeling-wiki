# Guthery and Bingham 2007

> [!summary] In plain words
> How to read regression models in wildlife science: what coefficients mean, how polynomial and interaction terms change interpretation, and why fitted equations are not mechanisms.

**Citation:** Guthery, F. S., and R. L. Bingham. 2007. A primer on interpreting regression models. *Journal of Wildlife Management* 71(3): 684 to 692.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 1, "Linear models and probability distributions".

## What it's about
Walks through interpreting slopes, intercepts, transformed and polynomial terms, interactions and asymptotic forms, with cautions about extrapolation and causal language.
