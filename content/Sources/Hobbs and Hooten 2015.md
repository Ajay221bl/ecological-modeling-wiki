# Hobbs and Hooten 2015

> [!summary] In plain words
> A primer that builds Bayesian hierarchical models from first principles: deterministic models, probability rules, likelihood, Bayes' theorem, hierarchical structure, MCMC and model checking.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Princeton University Press, Princeton, NJ.
**Syllabus week:** Ch1 to Ch9 are assigned across Sep 1 to Sep 22.

## What it's about
The book's thread is a line of inference: an ecological idea becomes a deterministic model, gets a probability model for the data, parameters and processes, and is fitted with MCMC. Each chapter has its own source note.
