# Hobbs and Hooten 2015 Ch2

> [!summary] In plain words
> Models start as deterministic functions that express ecological ideas. The chapter compares the three modeling styles in ecology and tours a "few good functions" used everywhere: linear, exponential, power, asymptotic and threshold.

**Citation:** Hobbs, N. T., and M. B. Hooten. 2015. *Bayesian Models: A Statistical Primer for Ecologists.* Ch. 2, "Deterministic Models", pp. 17 to 29. Princeton University Press.
**Syllabus week:** Tue Sep 1, "Linear models & probability distributions"

## What it's about
Every analysis asks "what is the probability I'd observe these data if the model faithfully represented the process?" (p. 17). The deterministic model is the starting point, and the next chapter adds probability.
