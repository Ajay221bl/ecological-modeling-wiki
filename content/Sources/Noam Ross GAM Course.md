# Noam Ross GAM Course

> [!summary] In plain words
> A free interactive online course on GAMs in R with mgcv, by Noam Ross: smooths, basis functions, smoothing parameters, checking, interactions and spatial smooths.

**Citation:** Ross, N. *GAMs in R* (free interactive course). https://noamross.github.io/gams-in-r-course/
**Syllabus week:** Tue Oct 27, "Generalized additive models" (lessons 1 and 2).

## What it's about
Lesson 1 introduces GAMs, basis functions, wiggliness and smoothing parameters; lesson 2 covers interpreting and visualising GAMs and checking them with gam.check() and concurvity. Later lessons cover spatial GAMs, interactions and logistic GAMs.
