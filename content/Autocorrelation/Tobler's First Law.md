# Tobler's First Law

**In one line:** Tobler's first law of geography says "everything is related to everything else, but near things are more related than distant things".

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Spatial Autocorrelation]] (what it describes)

## Start from zero
1. **From geographer Waldo Tobler (1970).**
2. **It is the plain-language statement of positive spatial autocorrelation.**
3. **McGill notes the same holds in ecology** across space, time and phylogeny.
4. **It is a tendency, not a law of nature.** Negative autocorrelation and barriers (rivers, mountains) break it.

## In the course
Opening line of the [[Dynamic Ecology Autocorrelation Post]].

## Common confusions
- **"It means distant things are unrelated."** They can still be related, just less.
