# Extent

**In one line:** extent is the total area (or time span) a study covers.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Spatial Scale]] (its parent concept)

## Start from zero
1. **Small extent:** one watershed. Large extent: a continent.
2. **Larger extents include more environmental variation** and more broad-scale gradients.
3. **Predictions beyond the extent are extrapolations.**
4. **Correlograms are usually read only up to about half the extent,** because far distance classes contain few pairs.

## In the course
[[Diniz-Filho et al. 2003]].

## Common confusions
- **"Extent and grain move together."** They are chosen separately.
