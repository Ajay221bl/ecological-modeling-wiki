# Super-population

**In one line:** the super-population is the total number of different animals that were in the study area at any time during the whole study, including those that entered or left between occasions.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Jolly-Seber and POPAN Models]] · [[Open Population]]

## The everyday picture
Everyone who ever used a gym during a year, rather than the number there on any one evening.

## Start from zero
1. **Always at least as big** as abundance on any occasion.
2. **POPAN estimates it directly;** robust-design models can derive it.
3. **With temporary emigration,** the super-population includes animals that use the area but were not on it during some sessions.
4. **Useful when** management cares about all animals that use a site, such as a stopover.

## In the course
[[Powell and Gale 2015]] Ch13 ("How can I estimate the size of a super-population?").

## Common confusions
- **"Super-population is average abundance."** It is the total of all distinct animals ever present.
