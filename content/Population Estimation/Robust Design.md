# Robust Design

**In one line:** the robust design combines closed and open sampling: several closely spaced secondary occasions within each primary period (treated as closed) and longer gaps between primary periods (open), so abundance, survival and temporary emigration can all be estimated.

> [!info] Before this, it helps to know
> [[Closed Population]] · [[Cormack-Jolly-Seber Model]]

## The everyday picture
Taking the class register several times in one lesson (closed) each week of term (open between weeks).

## Start from zero
1. **Pollock (1982)** proposed it.
2. **Within primary periods:** closed-capture models estimate N_t and p.
3. **Between primary periods:** open models estimate survival.
4. **Temporary emigration** is estimable because detection within periods is separated from presence.
5. **Robust to unequal catchability** in the closed part, hence the name.
6. **Multi-season occupancy has the same structure** at the species level.

## Common confusions
- **"The robust design is just more occasions."** Their arrangement into closed and open periods is what adds estimable parameters.
